This site provides step-by-step guides for setting up automated energy optimization with Home Assistant. The guides 
target Sigenergy battery systems with Amber Electric pricing in Australia, but the concepts apply to other setups.

By the end, you'll have Home Assistant automatically controlling your battery — charging when prices are low, 
discharging when prices are high, and maximizing your solar self-consumption. Think of it as a local, fully 
customizable alternative to Amber's SmartShift.

## Choose Your Optimizer

Both options use linear programming to find the optimal battery schedule. Choose based on your preference:

### HAEO (Recommended for new setups)

[HAEO](https://haeo.io/) is a native Home Assistant integration with UI-based configuration.

- Configure entirely through the Home Assistant UI
- Automatic re-optimization when forecasts change
- No YAML files or REST commands needed
- Designed for use with [HAFO](https://hafo.haeo.io/) for load forecasting
- Newer project, actively developed

[**Start the HAEO Guide →**](/haeo)

### EMHASS

[EMHASS](https://github.com/davidusb-geek/emhass) is a well-established Home Assistant addon.

- Mature and well-documented
- Large community and extensive configuration options
- Requires YAML configuration and REST commands
- Runs as a standalone addon

[**Start the EMHASS Guide →**](/emhass)
