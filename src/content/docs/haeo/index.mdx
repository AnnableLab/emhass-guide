Welcome to the HAEO guide. This guide will walk you through setting up [HAEO](https://haeo.io/) (Home Assistant Energy 
Optimizer) to automate your Sigenergy battery system.

## What You'll Set Up

By following this guide, you'll configure:

1. **HAEO** — The optimizer that creates your energy plan
2. **Input forecasts** — Price data from Amber, solar from Solcast, load from HAFO
3. **Dashboard** — Visualize your energy plan with ApexCharts
4. **Battery automation** — Automatically control your Sigenergy system based on the plan

## Why HAEO?

HAEO is a native Home Assistant integration designed for simplicity:

- **UI-based configuration** — No YAML files or REST commands
- **Automatic optimization** — Re-runs whenever your forecasts update
- **Native sensors** — Results appear as standard Home Assistant entities
- **Event-driven** — No need to schedule optimization runs
