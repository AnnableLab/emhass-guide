Despite its sophistication, HAEO is quite simple at its core. You configure 4 types of forecast inputs, and it 
produces an optimal strategy for your solar/battery system, including when to charge and discharge your battery.

Those 4 forecasts are:

1. A forecast of how much you can sell electricity for (i.e. feed-in price).
2. A forecast of how much you can buy electricity for.
3. A forecast of how much solar you will generate.
4. A forecast of how much electricity you will use.

HAEO takes all this information and generates an energy plan that is optimal for minimizing your energy costs. The 
optimization considers:

- When to charge the battery from the grid (buy low)
- When to discharge the battery to the grid (sell high)
- When to use solar directly vs store it
- When to self-consume vs export

As a native Home Assistant integration, HAEO automatically creates sensor entities with the optimization results. 
These sensors update automatically whenever your input forecasts change. You can then use automations to control your 
battery based on these sensors.

You can visualize this energy plan in a dashboard and see what HAEO has decided to do:

Here is a 3-day plan which minimizes cost. It will charge the battery during low-price periods (often overnight or 
during peak solar), and discharge during high-price periods (morning and evening peaks), keeping just enough battery 
capacity to last the night before starting over the next day.

### How It Works

1. **Input sensors** — HAEO reads forecast data from your configured sensors (Amber prices, Solcast solar, HAFO load)
2. **Optimization** — When inputs change, HAEO runs a linear programming optimization to find the lowest-cost plan
3. **Output sensors** — Results are published as Home Assistant sensors with current values and `forecast` attributes
4. **Automations** — Your automations read these sensors and control the battery accordingly

The entire process is automatic and event-driven. There's no need to schedule optimization runs — HAEO re-optimizes 
whenever relevant input data changes.
